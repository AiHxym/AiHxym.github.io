About Me

Yumeng Xue is a final-year Ph.D. candidate in the Visual Computing Group, Department of Computer and Information Science, University of Konstanz, expecting to graduate in March 2027. His supervisor is Prof. Dr. Oliver Deussen. In 2026 he was a visiting researcher at the VIDI Lab, University of California, Davis, hosted by Prof. Kwan-Liu Ma. He received his B.Eng. degree from Taishan College, Shandong University in 2020, and his M.Sc. degree (with distinction) from the University of Konstanz in 2022. His research interests fall in the field of Visualization, Visual Analytics, Human-Computer Interaction, and AI-Assisted Interactive Systems.
