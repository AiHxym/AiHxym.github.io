Yumeng Xue
Ph.D. Candidate at University of Konstanz

I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in the Visual Computing Group at the University of Konstanz, supervised by Prof. Dr. Oliver Deussen. My research focuses on visualizing large line ensembles and high-dimensional data, and on the interactive systems that make such data explorable. In 2026 I visited the VIDI Lab at UC Davis, hosted by Prof. Kwan-Liu Ma. 📊

Interests: Visualization, Visual Analytics, Human-Computer Interaction, and AI-Assisted Interactive Systems.
